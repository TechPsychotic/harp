[ 02 — Discover ]

Picks that
know you.

Harp keeps a small taste model on your device: what you finish, like, skip, and when. Smart Shuffle and the moods lean on the artists you love, and old habits fade so your picks move with you.

01 Smart Shuffle

Endless, and yours

Starts broad, then learns from every play.

02 Moods & charts

Chill, Focus, Party…

Moods tuned to the artists you like, charts from around the world.

03 Fair to songs

One skip isn't a verdict

A song only drops out after you skip it again and again.

04 Your files

Your own music, too

Files on your device play right alongside.

[ 03 — Offline ]

When the signal
lets go.

Download songs and whole playlists. While you listen, the next five songs are fetched onto your device, so skips start instantly and the music keeps going through tunnels and flights.

01 Downloads

Playlists, albums, likes

Downloaded songs never use data again.

02 Fetch ahead

Five songs ready

Fetched quietly, cleaned up after they've played.

03 Offline mode

Picks from what's here

Smart Shuffle works from your downloads alone.

[ 04 — Sound ]

Tuned to
your ears.

Pick your headphones from about 9,000 measured models and Harp corrects them to a neutral sound. Then shape it your way, keep volume even between songs, and let tracks flow gaplessly or crossfade.

01 AutoEq

Headphone correction

Measured profiles, applied with headroom so nothing clips.

02 Equalizer

10 bands and presets

Plus even volume (EBU R128) and playback speed.

03 Transitions

Gapless or crossfade

Albums flow; playlists blend, up to 12 seconds.

[ 06 — Together ]

Two devices,
one beat.

Start a party and send the link: friends hear the same moment and can play, pause or suggest the next song. Pair your phone and computer once and they stay in step. Direct, device to device.

01 Listen together

In sync to the beat

Kept within milliseconds, with an offset for Bluetooth.

02 Device sync

Pair once, done

Likes, playlists and history merge; nothing is overwritten.

03 Shared playlists

A link that stays fresh

Friends who save it get your changes.

02 Harp Encore

[ 09 — Privacy ]

No account. No ads.
Weightless.

Your history, taste and library live on your devices. Harp only talks to YouTube Music for songs, free lyrics sources, GitHub for updates, and, when you use them, iroh's relays to reach your own devices or friends. No analytics. No servers of ours.
