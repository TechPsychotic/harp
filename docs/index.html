SOUND

Sound

Tuned for your headphones.

Pick your headphones from about 9,000 measured models and Harp corrects them to a neutral sound, using AutoEq's measurements. Then shape it your way with a 10‑band equalizer, keep the volume even between songs, and let tracks flow gaplessly or crossfade.

  • Headphone correction (AutoEq)
  • 10‑band equalizer and presets
  • Even volume (EBU R128), gapless or crossfade
  • Wide stage, crossfeed and immersive modes; speed and sleep timer

TOGETHER

Peer to peer

Listen together. Sync everything. No account.

Start a party and send the link: friends hear the same song at the same moment, and anyone can play, pause or suggest the next track. Pair your phone and computer once and your likes, playlists and history stay in step. Share a playlist with a link and it stays up to date for everyone who saves it.

  • Listen together, kept in sync to the beat
  • Device sync that never overwrites anything
  • Shared playlists that follow your changes
  • Direct device‑to‑device, over iroh

Privacy

No account. No ads. No analytics.
No servers of ours.

Your history, taste and library live on your devices. Harp only talks to YouTube Music for search and streams, to free lyrics sources, to GitHub for updates, and, when you use them, to iroh's relays to reach your own devices or friends.
